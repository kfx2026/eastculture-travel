Guilin

East or West, Guilin Scenery is Best

Core Scenic Spots

Li River

The 83km cruise from Guilin to Yangshuo passes through the world's most famous karst landscape — limestone peaks rising dramatically from emerald waters. This scenery appears on the 20 RMB note.

Elephant Trunk Hill

Guilin's iconic landmark — a natural rock formation resembling an elephant drinking from the Li River. Best photographed at sunset.

Reed Flute Cave

A magnificent limestone cave filled with stalactites and stalagmites illuminated by colorful lights. Known as the 'Palace of Natural Arts'.

Longji Rice Terraces

Spectacular terraced fields built by Zhuang and Yao minorities over 650 years ago. The 'Dragon's Backbone' curves through the mountains like a giant staircase.

Local Cuisine

Guilin Rice Noodles

The city's signature dish — round rice noodles served in a rich bone broth with fried peanuts, pickled beans, and sliced beef. A 2000-year-old tradition.

Beer Fish

Fresh Li River fish braised in local beer with tomatoes, peppers, and garlic. A Yangshuo specialty.

Culture & Heritage

Zhuang Ethnic Culture

Guilin is home to China's largest ethnic minority — the Zhuang people, known for their colorful embroidery, folk songs, and the San Yue San festival.
