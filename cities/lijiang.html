Lijiang

Where Naxi Culture Meets Jade Dragon Snow Mountain

Core Scenic Spots

Old Town of Lijiang

A UNESCO World Heritage site — an 800-year-old town with cobblestone streets, wooden buildings, and canals fed by mountain springs. The Naxi people's ancient water system still flows through every alley.

Jade Dragon Snow Mountain

A 5596m snow-capped mountain sacred to the Naxi people. Take the cable car to 4506m for breathtaking views and the Impression Lijiang show at its base.

Tiger Leaping Gorge

One of the world's deepest gorges, where the Jinsha River thunders between Jade Dragon and Haba snow mountains. A legendary hiking destination.

Local Cuisine

Naxi Barbecue

Marinated pork belly grilled over charcoal with local spices. Crispy outside, tender inside — best enjoyed in a courtyard restaurant.

Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles

A Yunnan classic — piping hot broth served with raw ingredients that cook at the table. Legend says a scholar's wife invented it to keep his lunch warm.

Culture & Heritage

Naxi Dongba Culture

The Naxi people have preserved the world's last living pictographic writing system — Dongba script. Visit the Dongba Culture Museum to see this UNESCO Memory of the World.
