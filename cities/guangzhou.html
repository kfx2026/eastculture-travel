Guangzhou

The Southern Gateway — Cantonese Culture, Dim Sum Paradise

Core Scenic Spots

Canton Tower

At 600 meters, one of the world's tallest TV towers. The 'Slim Waist' offers observation decks, a skywalk, and the world's highest horizontal Ferris wheel.

Chen Clan Ancestral Hall

A magnificent 1894 complex showcasing Lingnan architecture with exquisite wood carvings, stone sculptures, and ceramic artistry. Now the Guangdong Folk Art Museum.

Shamian Island

A tranquil European-style enclave from the colonial era with tree-lined avenues and 19th-century buildings. A peaceful escape in the city center.

Local Cuisine

Dim Sum

Guangzhou is the birthplace of dim sum — bite-sized delicacies served in bamboo steamers. Har gow (shrimp dumplings) and siu mai are essential orders at any tea house.

White Cut Chicken

Poached chicken served at room temperature with ginger-scallion dip. The purest expression of Cantonese cooking — letting quality ingredients shine.

Wonton Noodles

Springy egg noodles in clear broth with plump shrimp wontons. A humble yet perfected Cantonese comfort food.

Culture & Heritage

Cantonese Opera

A UNESCO Intangible Cultural Heritage combining singing, martial arts, and elaborate costumes. Performed in Cantonese dialect with distinctive falsetto vocals.
