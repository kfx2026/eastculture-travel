Xi'an

The Eternal City - 13 Dynasties, One Terracotta Army

Core Scenic Spots

class="attraction-item">

Terracotta Army

The 8th Wonder of the World - over 8000 life-sized warriors, horses, and chariots buried with China's first emperor Qin Shi Huang in 210 BCE. Each face is unique, each warrior tells a story. Discovered by farmers in 1974, it remains one of the greatest archaeological finds in history.

Ancient City Wall

The most complete ancient city wall in China, built during the Ming Dynasty (1370). Rent a bike and cycle the 14km loop for panoramic views of old and new Xi'an. At night, the wall is illuminated with red lanterns, creating a magical atmosphere.

Muslim Quarter

A bustling food street in the heart of Xi'an's Muslim community, dating back to the Silk Road era. Sample lamb skewers, roujiamo (Chinese burger), and soak in the vibrant atmosphere of this living cultural crossroads.

Big Wild Goose Pagoda

A 64-meter Buddhist pagoda built in 652 AD to house scriptures brought from India by the monk Xuanzang. A symbol of the Silk Road and one of China's most iconic religious monuments.

Featured Cuisine

Roujiamo

Often called "Chinese hamburger" - slow-cooked shredded pork stuffed in freshly baked flatbread. Xi'an's most iconic street food with a history dating back to the Qin Dynasty.

Biangbiang Noodles

Wide hand-pulled noodles with a character so complex (58 strokes) it cannot be typed on a keyboard. Served with chili oil, garlic, and vegetables - a true taste of Shaanxi.

Yangrou Paomo

A hearty mutton soup where diners first break bread into tiny pieces, then the chef pours hot broth over it. A communal Xi'an tradition that brings people together around the table.

Culture & Heritage

Tang Dynasty Show

A spectacular performance recreating the music and dance of the Tang Dynasty (618-907), when Xi'an (then Chang'an) was the world's largest city with over one million residents.

Silk Road Heritage

Xi'an was the eastern terminus of the ancient Silk Road. The city's culture, cuisine, and architecture bear the imprint of centuries of trade connecting China to Central Asia, Persia, and beyond.

Travel Tips

Best time: Spring (March-May) and Autumn (September-November). High-speed rail connects Xi'an to Beijing (4.5hrs) and Chengdu (3hrs). The Terracotta Army is 40km east of the city - take the tourist bus from Xi'an Railway Station.
