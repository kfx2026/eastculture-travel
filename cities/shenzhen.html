Shenzhen

China's Silicon Valley — Innovation, Design, and Coastal Beauty

Core Scenic Spots

Window of the World

A theme park featuring 130 reproductions of world-famous landmarks at scale, from the Eiffel Tower to the Taj Mahal. Shenzhen's most iconic attraction.

Dameisha Beach

A popular public beach with golden sand and clear waters along Shenzhen's eastern coast. Free admission with excellent facilities.

Lianhua Mountain Park

A central urban park atop a hill with panoramic views of Shenzhen's skyline and a statue of Deng Xiaoping. A symbol of the city's transformation.

OCT-LOFT Creative Culture Park

A repurposed factory complex turned into a vibrant art district with galleries, design studios, cafes, and weekend markets. Shenzhen's creative heart.

Local Cuisine

Seafood

As a coastal city, Shenzhen excels in fresh seafood. Yantian Seafood Street offers hundreds of restaurants with live tanks — choose your fish, choose your cooking style.

Hakka Cuisine

Shenzhen's original settlers were Hakka people. Try stuffed tofu (yong tau foo) and salt-baked chicken for authentic local flavors.

Culture & Heritage

Maker Culture

Shenzhen is the world's hardware capital — where startups prototype in hours at Huaqiangbei electronics market. The city's identity is built on innovation.
