Xi'an

The Eternal City — 13 Dynasties, One Terracotta Army

Core Scenic Spots

Terracotta Army

The 8th Wonder of the World — over 8000 life-sized warriors, horses, and chariots buried with China's first emperor Qin Shi Huang in 210 BCE. Each face is unique.

Ancient City Wall

The most complete ancient city wall in China, built during the Ming Dynasty (1370). Rent a bike and cycle the 14km loop for panoramic views of old and new Xi'an.

Muslim Quarter

A bustling food street in the heart of Xi'an's Muslim community. Sample lamb skewers, roujiamo (Chinese burger), and yangrou paomo (bread in mutton soup).

Big Wild Goose Pagoda

A 64-meter Buddhist pagoda built in 652 AD to house scriptures brought from India by monk Xuanzang. A symbol of the Silk Road.

Local Cuisine

Roujiamo

Often called 'Chinese hamburger' — slow-cooked shredded pork stuffed in freshly baked flatbread. Xi'an's most iconic street food.

Biangbiang Noodles

Wide hand-pulled noodles with a character so complex (58 strokes) it can't be typed. Served with chili oil, garlic, and vegetables.

Yangrou Paomo

A hearty mutton soup where diners first break bread into tiny pieces, then the chef pours hot broth over it. A communal Xi'an tradition.

Culture & Heritage

Tang Dynasty Show

A spectacular performance recreating the music and dance of the Tang Dynasty (618-907), when Xi'an (then Chang'an) was the world's largest city.
