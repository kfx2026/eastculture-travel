Hangzhou

Heaven on Earth — Marco Polo's Most Beautiful City

Core Scenic Spots

West Lake

A UNESCO World Heritage site — the iconic lake surrounded by temples, pagodas, gardens, and arched bridges. The 'Ten Scenes of West Lake' have inspired poets for centuries.

Lingyin Temple

One of China's largest and wealthiest Buddhist temples, founded in 328 AD. The Feilai Feng grottoes contain 470 stone carvings dating from the 10th-14th centuries.

Longjing Tea Plantation

The birthplace of China's most famous green tea — Dragon Well (Longjing). Terraced fields on rolling hills where you can pick leaves and taste fresh brew.

Local Cuisine

Dongpo Pork

Braised pork belly named after poet Su Dongpo — melt-in-your-mouth tender with a caramelized soy glaze. A Hangzhou signature dish.

West Lake Vinegar Fish

Fresh grass carp from West Lake poached and dressed with sweet-sour vinegar sauce. Light and refreshing Hangzhou cuisine.

Culture & Heritage

Silk Culture

Hangzhou has been China's silk capital for over 1000 years. The China National Silk Museum is the world's largest silk museum.
