成都

Where ancient tea houses meet spicy street food, and giant pandas roam in misty bamboo forests. Chengdu is the ultimate escape into China's most laid-back yet vibrant soul.

Core Scenic Spots

Discover the must-visit attractions in 成都

Chengdu Research Base of Giant Panda Breeding
★★★★☆ (4.5)
Home to over 200 giant pandas and red pandas, this sprawling sanctuary nestled in the lush greenery of the northern suburbs offers an intimate glimpse into the lives of China's most beloved national treasures. Watch clumsy cubs tumble in the nurseries and adults lazily munch on bamboo.
📍 1375 Xiongmao Ave, Chenghua District 🕐 07:30 - 18:00 (Last entry 17:00) 💰 55 CNY (Peak season), 54 CNY (Off-season)

Tips: 1. Arrive by 7:30 AM to beat the crowds and see pandas eating breakfast. 2. Take the shuttle car directly to the furthest point (Moon Lake) and walk back down.

✨ The only place in the world to see giant panda cubs tumbling in the nursery.
Nature Wildlife Family
Wuhou Shrine
★★★★☆ (4.6)
A serene temple dedicated to Zhuge Liang and Liu Bei, the legendary heroes of the Three Kingdoms period. The shrine is a masterpiece of classical Chinese architecture, surrounded by ancient cypress trees, red-wall corridors, and the famous Red Cliff Stele Pavilion.
📍 231 Wuhouci Main St, Wuhou District 🕐 08:00 - 18:00 (May-Oct), 08:00 - 18:30 (Nov-Apr) 💰 50 CNY

Tips: 1. Visit the adjacent Jinli Street right after for a quick stroll, but don't eat the overpriced snacks there. 2. Rent an audio guide to understand the complex Three Kingdoms history.

✨ Walk through the iconic crimson red-wall alleyway lined with swaying bamboo.
History Architecture Culture
Du Fu Thatched Cottage
★★★★☆ (4.7)
The former residence of China's greatest realist poet, Du Fu, from the Tang Dynasty. This tranquil garden estate features classical pavilions, lotus ponds, and ancient plum trees, offering a poetic retreat from the bustling city.
📍 37 Qinghua Rd, Qingyang District 🕐 08:00 - 18:00 💰 50 CNY

Tips: 1. Visit the Bonsai Garden and the Plum Garden if you are there in early spring. 2. Grab a cup of tea at the traditional teahouse inside the park.

✨ Experience the profound poetic atmosphere of a Tang Dynasty scholar's garden.
Poetry Garden Heritage
Dujiangyan Irrigation System
★★★★☆ (4.8)
An ancient engineering marvel built in 256 BC that is still in use today, controlling the Minjiang River without a dam. Surrounded by lush mountains and the sacred Mount Qingcheng, it stands as a testament to ancient Chinese wisdom.
📍 Dujiangyan City, Chengdu (60km NW of downtown) 🕐 08:00 - 17:30 💰 80 CNY

Tips: 1. Take the high-speed train from Xipu Station in Chengdu (30 mins). 2. Enter from the Qin Yan Lou gate to walk across the Anlan Suspension Bridge.

✨ Witness a 2,200-year-old engineering marvel that still feeds the fertile Chengdu Plain.
Engineering UNESCO Nature
People's Park
★★★★☆ (4.5)
The beating heart of Chengdu's laid-back lifestyle. Here, locals gather to drink tea, get their ears cleaned, play Mahjong, and dance. The Heming Teahouse is the epicenter of this vibrant, unpretentious slice of local life.
📍 129 Shaocheng Ave, Qingyang District 🕐 Open 24 hours (Teahouse 06:00 - 21:00) 💰 Free (Tea 20-50 CNY)

Tips: 1. Try the unique 'ear cleaning' service (Cai Er) for about 50 CNY. 2. Go on a Sunday morning to witness the local matchmaking corner.

✨ Sip jasmine tea in a bamboo chair while watching locals master the art of 'doing nothing'.
Local Life Teahouse Relaxing
Taste

Local Cuisine

Savor the authentic flavors of 成都

Sichuan Hotpot (四川火锅)
A bubbling cauldron of spicy, numbing beef tallow broth infused with Sichuan peppercorns. Ingredients like tripe, duck blood, and lotus root are swished and cooked at the table, creating a fiery, communal feast.
📍 Xiaolongkan Hotpot (for authentic local vibes) or Shoo Loong Kan (for premium service). 💰 100 - 150 CNY per person
💡 Order 'Wei La' (mild spicy) if you can't handle heat. Must-dip: crispy tripe (Mao Du) for exactly 15 seconds.
Chuanchuan Xiang (串串香)
The street-food sibling of hotpot. Skewers of meat, vegetables, and tofu are cooked in a spicy broth. You grab a handful of sticks from the fridge, boil them, and pay by the weight of the bamboo sticks.
📍 Yulin Chuanchuan or Ganggang Hushi. 💰 60 - 90 CNY per person
💡 Look for the small quail egg skewers and marinated beef. Pair it with a cold 'Ice Powder' (Bing Fen) to cool your palate.
Dan Dan Noodles (担担面)
A legendary street snack carried by shoulder poles in the past. Thin noodles are smothered in a rich, spicy sauce made with preserved mustard greens, chili oil, and crushed peanuts.
📍 Chen Mapo Tofu (surprisingly good noodles) or any local hole-in-the-wall in Yulin. 💰 15 - 25 CNY per bowl
💡 Mix it vigorously before eating to coat every strand. Add a dash of black vinegar for an extra kick.
Mapo Tofu (麻婆豆腐)
The ultimate test of Sichuan cuisine's 'Ma' (numbing) and 'La' (spicy). Silky tofu cubes simmered in a fiery broad bean paste sauce with minced beef, topped with a tingle-inducing dust of Sichuan peppercorn.
📍 Chen Mapo Tofu Restaurant (the original birthplace since 1862). 💰 30 - 50 CNY per dish
💡 Order it with a bowl of plain white rice. The sauce is meant to be spooned over the rice.
Culture

Culture & Heritage

Experience the cultural soul of 成都

Chengdu Teahouse Culture

More than just drinking tea, it's a social institution. The 'Gaiwan' (lidded bowl) represents heaven, earth, and man. Locals spend hours here gossiping, playing cards, and embracing the philosophy of 'Bashi' (comfortable).

Significance: It embodies the Chengdu ethos of slowing down and finding joy in the mundane.

Best Time: Any afternoon, especially on a sunny weekend.

How to experience: Book a bamboo chair at Heming Teahouse, order a jasmine tea, and watch the tea masters perform the traditional water-pouring art.

Sichuan Opera Face-Changing

A mesmerizing theatrical art where performers magically swap colorful, painted masks in the blink of an eye, representing different emotions and characters. It's often accompanied by fire-breathing and shadow puppetry.

Significance: A unique intangible cultural heritage that reveals the dramatic flair of Sichuan's local culture.

Best Time: Evening performances at Shufeng Yayun or Jinli Theater.

How to experience: Buy a front-row ticket at Shufeng Yayun, enjoy the complimentary tea, and watch the masters perform just inches from your face.

Travel Guide

Everything you need to plan your trip

Climate

Subtropical monsoon climate. Summers are hot, humid, and muggy (often over 30°C/86°F). Winters are damp, chilly, and lack central heating. Spring and Autumn are the most pleasant, though rain is frequent year-round.

Recommended Duration

4-5 Days. (2 days for city highlights, 1 day for Dujiangyan/Qingchengshan, 1 day for food exploration).

Local Transit

Excellent Metro system covers most attractions. Use Alipay/WeChat to scan QR codes for entry. DiDi (ride-hailing) is cheap and convenient.

Getting Here

Tianfu (TFU) and Shuangliu (CTU) airports serve global/domestic flights. Chengdu East Station is a major high-speed rail hub (e.g., 4 hrs to Xi'an, 8 hrs to Beijing).

Travel Tips

Download offline maps. Bring an umbrella regardless of the season. Learn to say 'Bu Yao La' (No spice) if your stomach can't handle Sichuan heat.
Insights

Deep Travel Insights

Honest advice and local knowledge from experienced travelers

Practical Guide

Visa: Chengdu offers a 144-hour visa-free transit for citizens of 53 countries. Payment: Cash is rarely used; link your foreign credit card to Alipay or WeChat Pay before arrival. SIM Card: Buy a tourist SIM at the airport or use an eSIM like Airalo for data. Language: English is limited outside major hotels; download a translation app.

Honest Tips

The Panda Base Reality: If you arrive after 9:00 AM, you will see pandas sleeping in hidden enclosures and fight through shoulder-to-shoulder crowds. You must arrive at 7:30 AM sharp to see them eating. It is a zoo, not a wilderness sanctuary.

Tourist Traps: Kuanzhai Alley (Wide and Narrow Alleys) and Jinli Street are beautifully lit at night but are essentially overpriced food courts selling identical, mediocre snacks to tourists. Skip the snacks there; eat in the residential alleys instead.

The Spice Warning: 'Mild' (Wei La) in Chengdu is 'Extremely Hot' everywhere else. Do not trust the spice levels. If you have a sensitive stomach, buy medicine at a local pharmacy beforehand, because the chili oil will find you.

Local Secrets

Yulin Neighborhood: Skip the commercialized bars of Jiuyanqiao. Head to the Yulin neighborhood (made famous by a local folk song) for authentic hole-in-the-wall BBQ, local craft beer, and genuine Chengdu indie music vibes.

The South Gate Secret: For the Panda Base, most tourists enter the South Gate. If you want a quieter experience with the red pandas, try the newer expansion areas or visit the Dujiangyan Panda Base, which is less crowded and equally fascinating.

Hidden Teahouse: Instead of the crowded People's Park, take a taxi to Wenshu Monastery. The teahouse in the courtyard is surrounded by ancient trees and incense smoke, offering a profoundly spiritual and quiet tea experience for just 15 CNY.
