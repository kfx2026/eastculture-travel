Qingdao

Sailing City — German Heritage, Tsingtao Beer, and Golden Beaches

Core Scenic Spots

Zhanqiao Pier

Qingdao's iconic 440m pier extending into the Yellow Sea with an octagonal pavilion at its end. Built in 1891, it's the city's most recognizable landmark.

Badaguan Scenic Area

A historic district with tree-lined streets named after ancient military passes, featuring over 200 villas in German, Russian, British, and French architectural styles.

Laoshan Mountain

A sacred Taoist mountain rising 1133m above the sea — the highest coastal mountain in China. Ancient temples dot its misty peaks with ocean views.

Tsingtao Beer Museum

Housed in the original 1903 German brewery, this museum tells the story of China's most famous beer. Includes tastings of freshly brewed draft beer.

Local Cuisine

Tsingtao Beer & Seafood

The classic Qingdao experience — fresh clams, grilled squid, and steamed crabs washed down with ice-cold Tsingtao draft beer. Best enjoyed at a beachside shack.

Lu Cuisine (Shandong)

One of China's Eight Great Cuisines — known for braised dishes, seafood, and clear soups. Try braised sea cucumber and sweet-sour carp.

Culture & Heritage

International Beer Festival

Asia's largest beer festival held annually in August, attracting millions of visitors. A month-long celebration of beer, music, and seaside fun.
