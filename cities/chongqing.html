Chongqing

Mountain City — Hot Pot Capital on the Yangtze

Core Scenic Spots

Hongya Cave

An 11-story stilt house complex clinging to a cliff above the Jialing River. At night, its traditional architecture glows like a scene from Spirited Away.

Ciqikou Ancient Town

A 1000-year-old porcelain trading town with narrow stone alleys, traditional workshops, and countless snack vendors. The best place to experience old Chongqing.

Dazu Rock Carvings

A UNESCO World Heritage site featuring 50,000 Buddhist, Taoist, and Confucian rock carvings from the 9th-13th centuries. A masterpiece of Chinese religious art.

Yangtze River Cableway

An iconic 1166m cable car crossing the Yangtze River, offering stunning views of the city's layered skyline. A must-ride Chongqing experience.

Local Cuisine

Chongqing Hot Pot

The soul of the city — a bubbling cauldron of fiery red chili oil and Sichuan peppercorns. Dip everything from tripe to lotus root. The numbing heat is addictive.

Chongqing Noodles

A simple yet legendary street food — wheat noodles in spicy chili oil with minced pork, pickled vegetables, and crushed peanuts. The perfect breakfast.

Jiangtuan Fish

A prized Yangtze River fish known for its tender, boneless meat. Typically steamed with ginger and scallions to preserve the delicate flavor.

Culture & Heritage

Sichuan Opera Face-Changing

Chongqing shares this UNESCO heritage with Chengdu. The lightning-fast mask changes are a mesmerizing performance art unique to the region.
