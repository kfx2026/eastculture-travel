Ya'an

The Rain City - Gateway to Western Sichuan and Panda Country

Core Scenic Spots

class="attraction-item">

Bifengxia Panda Base

The largest giant panda breeding and research center in the world, nestled in lush green valleys. Home to over 80 pandas in semi-wild enclosures surrounded by bamboo forests and waterfalls.

Shangli Ancient Town

A well-preserved Ming-Qing Dynasty town with stone bridges, wooden stilt houses, and cobblestone streets. Known as the ink-wash painting town of western Sichuan for its timeless beauty.

Mengding Mountain

The birthplace of Chinese tea culture - tea has been cultivated here for over 2000 years. Visit terraced tea plantations and ancient tea trees on misty mountain slopes.

Featured Cuisine

Ya Fish (Yayu)

A legendary cold-water fish found only in Ya'an's pristine rivers, prized for its tender, flavorful meat. Typically steamed with ginger and scallions to preserve its delicate taste.

Mengding Tea

One of China's most historically significant teas, once sent as tribute to emperors. Sample freshly roasted green tea at mountain plantations overlooking the misty valleys.

Culture & Heritage

Tea-Horse Road Heritage

Ya'an was a crucial hub on the ancient Tea-Horse Road connecting Sichuan to Tibet. The city preserves centuries-old tea processing traditions and the spirit of this legendary trade route.

Travel Tips

Best time: Spring and Autumn. Ya'an is famous for its rain - bring an umbrella. High-speed rail connects to Chengdu in just 1 hour. The Bifengxia Panda Base is a 30-minute drive from downtown.
