Suzhou

Venice of the East — Classical Gardens and Silk Canals

Core Scenic Spots

Humble Administrator's Garden

The largest and most famous classical garden in Suzhou, built in 1509. A UNESCO World Heritage site with pavilions, ponds, and meticulously designed landscapes.

Tiger Hill

A 36m hill with the 1000-year-old leaning Yunyan Pagoda. Known as the 'No.1 Sight of Suzhou' with history spanning 2500 years.

Pingjiang Road

A 1600-year-old canal-side street lined with traditional white-walled buildings, teahouses, and silk shops. Suzhou's best-preserved historic neighborhood.

Local Cuisine

Squirrel-shaped Mandarin Fish

Deep-fried mandarin fish carved to resemble a squirrel, drizzled with sweet-sour sauce. Suzhou's most famous dish with stunning presentation.

Suzhou-style Noodles

Delicate noodles in clear broth with various toppings like braised pork, shrimp, and seasonal vegetables. A comforting breakfast staple.

Culture & Heritage

Kunqu Opera

One of the oldest forms of Chinese opera, born in Suzhou 600 years ago. UNESCO Masterpiece of Oral and Intangible Heritage with graceful movements and poetic lyrics.

Silk Embroidery

Suzhou embroidery is considered the finest in China, with double-sided embroidery being the pinnacle — both sides show different images with no visible knots.
